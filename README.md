# AtliQ Hardware - Excel Sales & Finance Analytics

## Overview
AtliQ Hardware is a leading computer hardware manufacturer with operations across multiple markets. This project provides an end-to-end Sales and Finance Analytics solution built in **Microsoft Excel** 

The objective was to build executive-level reports to track business performance and support data-driven decision making.

> **Disclaimer:** Dataset is provided by Codebasics.io for learning purposes. This repository contains only report PDFs (outputs), not the raw dataset.

## Business Objective
To analyze AtliQ Hardware's sales data and answer key business questions related to:
- Financial Performance (P&L Statement)
- Customer & Market Performance
- Product Performance & New Product Launches
- Sales Target Achievement

## Repository Structure
This repository contains **11 individual reports**, each exported as a single PDF for easy viewing (as shown in the attached videos).

### A. Finance Analytics
- **P&L by Fiscal Year:** Net Sales, COGS, Gross Margin, and GM% trend for FY 2019-2021
- **P&L by Months:** Monthly and Quarterly P&L to identify seasonality
- **P&L by Markets:** Country-wise P&L with profitability analysis
- **GM% by Quarters:** Gross Margin percentage trend

### B. Sales & Market Analytics
- **Customer Performance Report:** Customer-wise Net Sales (2019-2021) and YoY Growth %
- **Market Performance vs Target:** Actual vs Target comparison for 2021 with variance analysis
- **Top 5 Country - 2021:** Top 5 countries by revenue in 2021

### C. Product Analytics
- **Top 10 Products:** Best-selling products by revenue with growth percentage
- **Top & Bottom 5 Products:** Identification of best and worst performing products
- **New Products - 2021:** Performance analysis of 17 new products launched in 2021

## Key Insights

1.  **Strong Growth:** Net Sales increased by 124.8% in FY21 vs FY20
2.  **Market Leaders:** USA ($87.2M) and India ($50M) are the top 2 markets, contributing over 55% of total sales
3.  **Target Achievement:** USA, India, Canada, and South Korea exceeded 2021 sales targets, while European markets underperformed
4.  **Top Product:** AQ Master wired x1 Ms - Achieved $4.2M in sales with 1649% YoY growth, making it the best performer
5.  **Product Risk:** AQ HOME Allin1 series featured in the bottom 5 products, requiring strategic review
6.  **New Launches:** 17 new products launched in 2021 generated $1.74M in sales

## Tools & Techniques Used
- **Tool:** Microsoft Excel
- **Techniques:** Pivot Tables, Advanced Formulas (SUMIFS, INDEX-MATCH), Conditional Formatting (Heatmaps), P&L Structuring, Data Visualization, Report Formatting
